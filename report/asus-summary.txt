Subject: ROG Strix G18 G814JZ: audio crackle on USB and internal speakers under gaming load; stops when the middle fan runs faster

Model: ROG Strix G18 G814JZ, i9-13980HX, RTX 4080, BIOS G814JZ.334. OS: Linux (Fedora 45), fan control through the standard ASUS WMI fan-curve interface.

Problem
After roughly 15-20 minutes of gaming, audio crackles on a USB audio interface and on the internal speakers. Audio over HDMI from the NVIDIA card is not affected.

What I measured
- The chipset's USB controller fails to send about 9.5 % of the audio packets (it reports them as having missed their time slot), in bursts of about 1 ms, around 84 times per second.
- The chipset's own audio controller reports data underruns at the same rate at the same time.
- The rate follows board temperature and fan speed, not CPU or GPU load. With the default Performance fan curve the fans top out at 84 % (CPU), 69 % (GPU) and 43 % (middle fan), and a board temperature sensor (ACPI thermal zone SEN3) reaches 87-91 C. Its own trip points are 65, 75 and 80 C.
- Running the middle fan at 100 % (7400 rpm instead of 4300) and the GPU fan at 100 % above 80 C stops the problem completely, with the same game and the same GPU power.

Questions
1. Is the chipset on this model expected to throttle or stall when that area of the board reaches these temperatures?
2. Is the default Performance curve for the middle fan intended to stop at 43 %? Could a BIOS update raise it?
3. Is there a known issue with thermal pads or cooling around the chipset on this model?

I can provide logs and a USB capture if useful.
